FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Sepnexus really invitation-only?

Yes. We onboard a small number of customers per quarter so each deployment gets the architecture and operations attention it deserves. Request access and we'll get back within 48 hours.

What happens after the pilot?

We graduate to Operate or Sovereign with zero re-platforming. Your agents, data and integrations carry over — the difference is isolation, scale and SLA.

Can we bring our own cloud?

On Sovereign, yes. We deploy into your AWS, GCP or Azure account via Terraform and operate it under our SLA. Your data never leaves your perimeter.

How is billing measured?

Flat platform fee + metered agent runs above your tier. No surprise charges — every run is logged with cost attribution in your dashboard.

Do you sign DPAs / MSAs?

Standard on Sovereign. Available on Operate by request. Our security packet is ready when your procurement team is.
